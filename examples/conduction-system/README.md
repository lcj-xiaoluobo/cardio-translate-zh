## 英文原文

Figure 1. A simplified view of the cardiac conduction system. Labels identify the sinoatrial node, atrioventricular node, His bundle, and right and left bundle branches. Right and left refer to the patient's anatomical sides, not the sides of the page.

Each leader line identifies a single structure. If a label is too long to fit on one line, it may be split into two lines without moving the leader line or its endpoint. A shorter translation should not be used if it changes the anatomical meaning. The diagram illustrates the relationship between labeled structures; it does not show their exact dimensions.

## 中文定稿

图1　心脏传导系统简化示意图。图中标示窦房结、房室结、希氏束及左右束支。左、右均指患者的解剖侧别，而非页面的左右方向。

每条引线仅指向一个结构。标签过长、无法单行排下时，可分为两行，但应保持引线及其端点位置不变。不得为缩短译文而改变解剖含义。本图仅示意所标结构之间的关系，不反映其精确尺寸。
